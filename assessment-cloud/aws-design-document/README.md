## Coverage

1. Cloud architecture and environment isolation.
2. Security controls and access models.
3. Networking and private connectivity.
4. Observability, logging, tracing, and alerting.
5. Developer experience and self-service.
6. Cost management and the cost-growth investigation scenario.
7. AI governance, approval, audit, and emergency controls.

The document explains the platform design. Recovery objectives and retention periods
are proposed assumptions pending business approval. Implementation scope and local
checks are documented in the [Terraform guide](../aws-terraform-vault/README.md).
